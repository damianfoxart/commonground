# Content Pipeline & Safety Rails

## Purpose of This Document

This document defines **how new content is added** to *Common Ground* without breaking tone, lore, or systems.

It exists to prevent:
- Narrative drift
- Accidental lore contradictions
- Mechanics being added without thematic justification
- “Cool idea” creep that undermines the core experience

If content cannot pass these rails, it does not enter the game.

---

## Core Principle

**All content must serve at least ONE of the following:**
1. Environmental restoration
2. Character relationships
3. Thematic reinforcement (care, compromise, doubt, responsibility)

If it serves none of these, it is cut.

---

## Narrative Safety Rails

### Tone Guardrails
- The game remains **gentle, melancholic, and quietly unsettling**
- Humor is allowed, but must be:
  - Character-driven
  - Never cynical
  - Never mocking the player’s care or effort
- Horror exists only as **implication**, not spectacle

No:
- Snark
- Meta jokes about “games”
- Grimdark indulgence
- Shock-for-shock’s-sake twists

---

### Lore Integrity Rules
- Gods do **not** appear casually
- Divine intent is indirect, mediated, or inferred
- Exuvians do not explain the cosmology
- Mortals never fully understand divine systems

If a scene explains too much, it must be obscured, fragmented, or reframed.

---

### Character Use Rules
- Characters act according to their **core emotional truth**
- No character exists solely to deliver exposition
- No character may “know more than they reasonably should”

If a character must reveal information:
- It should be partial
- Biased
- Or emotionally motivated

---

## Mechanical Safety Rails

### Systems Before Features
- No new mechanic may be added without:
  - A narrative justification
  - A clear interaction with existing systems
- Mechanics should **interlock**, not stack independently

If a mechanic cannot touch Spectra, the village, or the world state, reconsider it.

---

### Player Agency Protection
- The player is never punished for:
  - Caring
  - Experimenting
  - Acting gently
- Failure states should:
  - Redirect
  - Complicate
  - Escalate pressure  
  Not hard-stop progress

---

### Visibility Rule
- Major consequences should be:
  - Felt before they are explained
  - Seen before they are named

Avoid pop-ups, alerts, or overt warnings.
Let the world react first.

---

## Content Approval Checklist

Before adding content, confirm:

- [ ] Does this reinforce the game’s themes?
- [ ] Does it respect established lore rules?
- [ ] Does it avoid explaining divine systems too clearly?
- [ ] Does it preserve the game’s gentle surface tone?
- [ ] Does it interact with existing mechanics meaningfully?

If any answer is “no,” revise or discard.

---

## What This Game Is Protecting

*Common Ground* is not about winning.
It is about **choosing to care in a system that profits from indifference**.

All content must protect that experience.
