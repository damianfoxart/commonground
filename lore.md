# Lore & World Rules

## Purpose of This Document

This document exists to **lock the cosmology** of *Common Ground*.

It defines what is true about the world, how divine systems operate, and which assumptions must never be violated.  
All narrative, mechanics, and future content must remain compatible with these rules.

If something conflicts with this document, **the new idea is wrong** unless explicitly revised here.

---

## The World

- The world is measured in ecological metrics that rank stability.
- History is not clean or cyclical — it is fractured, buried, and partially erased.
- The players attempts at healing the land through ecological stability metrics will reveal many of the secrets of the past.
- Progress and restoration draw attention from forces that prefer stasis or control.

---

## Exuvia

- **Exuvia is a parallel state of existence**, not a simple afterlife.
- All beings that cross into Exuvia become undead in some form.
- Exuvians are not united; they are defined by perpetual internal conflict.
- Violence is the primary language Exuvians understand.
- Exuvians are often guarding systems or locations they do not fully understand.
- Exuvia is sustained by belief, narrative saturation, and divine accounting.

---

## The Gods (High-Level Structure)

There are **48 gods**, divided into four domains:

### Gods of Law
- Former scientists who became embodiments of physical and conceptual laws.
- Obsessed with determinism, control, and systemic perfection.
- Believe the universe can be fully known and managed.
- Rely on misinformation to cover anomalies and mistakes.

### Gods of Virtue (Paladins)
- Former mortals who ascended through sacrifice and idealism.
- Represent abstract moral principles taken to absolute extremes.
- Genuinely believe in what they represent.
- Drift toward detachment and dogma over time.

### Gods of Vice (Demons)
- Distorted reflections of the Virtues.
- Represent moral failure and excess, not chaos.
- Exist as shadows and consequences of rigid idealism.

### Gods of Chaos (Constellations)
- Elemental, mythic, and indifferent.
- Concerned with balance, flow, and natural math.
- Do not care about mortal intent — only inputs and outputs.

---

## Divine Power & Belief

- **Belief creates divinity**, not the reverse.
- Divine power is finite and divided among all active gods.
- When a god is destroyed or removed, remaining gods gain proportionally more power.
- Gods cannot easily act directly in the mortal world.
- Divine intervention always carries systemic cost.

---

## OATHS

- OATHS are utilitarian power interfaces, not faith-based prayers.
- Mortals invoke gods through OATHS to access power.
- Gods do not always receive a clear “signal” when an OATH is made.
- Chaos gods often do not care about OATHS at all.
- Exuvians **do not make OATHS** — they already belong to a god.
- OATHS bind both parties; possession is a two-way tether.

---

## The Divine Engine (Armory)

- The Divine Engine predates all modern gods.
- It was used to destroy Abraxas, a monotheistic deity.
- Its activation caused catastrophic reality damage.
- The engine **cannot be safely approached by gods**.
- Proximity to the engine destabilizes divine beings.
- The engine manifests the *exact will* of its activator.
- It scans identity at a fundamental, molecular level.
- The engine is not destroyed because:
  - The gods fear competition.
  - They cannot safely approach or possess mortals near it.
  - Its existence is covered up through misinformation.

---

## Rexen Religious Law (Locked)

- Rexen religion predates humanity.
- Stonehaven was a **holy site**, not a settlement.
- Rexen strictly forbid recording words or images.
- This taboo exists because they witnessed:
  - The use of the Divine Engine.
  - The destruction of Abraxas.
  - The birth of the gods of Law.
- Rexen doctrine exists to prevent this path from ever being walked again.

---

## The Divine Casino

- Gods treat mortals as game pieces.
- Souls function as wagers.
- Divine “chips” can swap positions on the board.
- Rifts are artifacts of divine movement and cheating.
- The system is inherently unstable.
- Discordia maintains the illusion of perfection.

---

## Discordia (Locked Interpretation)

- Discordia is the Goddess of Misinformation.
- In life, she was a public relations manager, not a scientist.
- She exists to clean up divine mistakes.
- She is resented and mistreated by other Law gods.
- She falsifies records to preserve belief in divine perfection.
- She tolerates chaos if it destabilizes the system.
- She is indifferent to virtue and vice.
- She does not intervene to stop Teo.
- She benefits from gods destroying one another.

---

## Things That Are Locked (Do Not Change Without Revision)

- Gods are not omniscient.
- Gods are not unified.
- Belief is the source of divinity.
- The Divine Engine cannot be safely destroyed by gods.
- Exuvians are already bound to gods.
- Stonehaven exists in a dangerous, politically sensitive void.
- Success invites pressure.
- No faction is secretly “the good guys.”

---

## Canon Rule

If a future idea:
- Removes consequence
- Makes gods infallible
- Turns sacrifice into a clean transaction
- Allows easy divine intervention

…it contradicts *Common Ground*.
